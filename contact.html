Last page of the sketchbook

If you want to build something

Then this is the page you came for. I am still early in my career and I say so everywhere else on this site, so I would rather be direct about what I am after than pretend to be further along.

Some projects begin as a sketch. The rest begin with a conversation.

What I am after

Where I would genuinely use a reply

Mentorship

Someone further along who will look at my code honestly. Especially on the back end, which is where I am weakest and most keen.

Work and internships

Junior front-end or full-stack roles, and anything junior enough that needing guidance is expected rather than a problem.

Collaboration

Team projects especially. Working with other developers is the fastest thing that has happened to my code.

Where to find me

Both of these are real and both are monitored, so either one reaches me properly. LinkedIn is better for anything with a person in it; GitHub is better for anything about the code.

About a message form

There is no form on this page, and that is deliberate rather than an oversight.

This is a static site with no server behind it. A contact form here would either silently fail or invent a mail address that does not exist, and both are worse than telling you where to actually find me.

So: use LinkedIn or GitHub. If a form matters to you for what you are sending, say so in the message and I will give you a way that works.

One last thing

I am not going to pretend this portfolio makes me a strong candidate. It is seventeen weeks of work and a lot of wrong turns, and you can see all of them.

What I can tell you is that I show up, that I would rather fix something than defend it, and that I care about the part of the code nobody sees. If that is useful to you, the door is open.

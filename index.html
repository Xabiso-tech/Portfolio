Why the drawing matters

A blank page and a loaded page behave nothing alike. That gap is where the work lives.

I got into web development the long way round, through design and drawing, and I never really left it behind. It shows up as the reason I still sketch a layout before I write it, the reason I care about a border-radius and a line-height, and the reason I think a page should feel like somebody made it rather than like something was generated.

So this portfolio is built the way I would build anything. Work in small marks. Check the measurements. Keep a reference sheet. Revise until the construction lines disappear and only the drawing is left. The animations are no different: every line here is drawn by hand, and nothing moves that does not need to.

E-COMMERCE · TEAM DEVELOPMENT

Connecting the moving parts.

WeConnect is an e-commerce project focused on connecting small businesses and suppliers. The wider platform includes workflows around orders and deliveries, with tracking and location-related functionality forming part of the project.

It provides a practical context for exploring how user interfaces, backend services, databases, and business workflows fit together into a web application.

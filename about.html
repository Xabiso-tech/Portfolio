STOP 01

THE DEPARTURE POINT

When the expected route didn't open

For a while, my future felt tied to the conventional careers I had grown up hearing about — becoming a doctor, teacher, or lawyer. But I couldn't get into university, and repeated attempts to pass Mathematics and Physical Sciences didn't work out the way I hoped. I found myself unemployed, unsure of what my next step would be.

Before Life Choices Academy, I knew coding existed, but I didn't really understand what it involved. It wasn't a lifelong plan or some perfectly mapped-out destination. It was a road I hadn't properly explored yet.

STOP 02

THE FIRST SPARK

Wait… coding is actually fun?

After applying to Life Choices Academy, I needed to prepare for the assessment. I downloaded a Python learning app that taught through quizzes and questions, and I started working my way through them.

Something clicked. Coding felt like playing a game: try something, work through a challenge, learn from the result, and move on to the next one. What had been unfamiliar became something I genuinely wanted to explore.

STOP 03

ROADWORKS & REAL LESSONS

The day GitHub taught me to slow down

One mistake made the importance of version control very real. While trying to edit my work, I accidentally deleted our team's work on GitHub because I was working directly on the main branch instead of my own branch.

I asked my team leader for help, and she helped me fix the mistake. It was stressful, but it taught me something I couldn't afford to treat casually: branches help people work separately, protect shared work, and bring changes together more safely.

I learned to pay closer attention to where I'm working, understand the tools before using them, and ask for help when I need it. Perseverance doesn't mean never making mistakes. It means learning from them and continuing forward.

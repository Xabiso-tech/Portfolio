FIELD NOTE01THE DEPARTURE

THE ROUTE I THOUGHT I WOULD TAKE

When the expected route didn't open

For a long time, I imagined a conventional future: doctor, teacher, or lawyer. I couldn't get into university, and my attempts to rewrite Mathematics and Physical Sciences didn't work out. I eventually found myself unemployed, unsure what came next.

Coding was something I knew existed, but I had little idea what it involved. It wasn't a lifelong plan. It was a direction I hadn't properly explored yet.

IN THE MARGIN

The map changed before I knew where I was going.

THE FIRST SPARK

Wait, coding is actually fun?

Before my Life Choices Academy assessment, I downloaded a Python learning app that taught through quizzes. I worked through one question, then another. Coding started to feel like a game: try something, see what happens, learn from the result, and try again.

That was the first time I wanted to keep exploring what code could do.

NOTES FROM ALONG THE WAY

What travels with me

Code is one part of my life. The way I see people and the world around me shapes what I hope to do with it.

01 / PEOPLE

A gentle heart, an endlessly curious mind.

I'm soft-spoken and care deeply about how people are treated. I try not to reduce someone to what they struggle with; we all have different strengths and limitations. Respect, humility, love, and compassion matter to me, and I feel most like myself when I'm helping someone.

I love conversations that go past small talk, about ideas, feelings, people, and why we see things differently. I can get absorbed in a question. My sense of humour sometimes needs a second explanation.

SMALL OBSERVATION

Understanding someone often starts with listening long enough to be surprised.

✳

THE FOUNDATION I RETURN TO

My faith in Christ is central to who I am.

It shapes how I understand love, humility, compassion, and the way I want to treat people. Serving through church events and evangelising activities is one way I try to live that out. Faith is part of the foundation for the life I want to lead and the work I hope to do.

SKETCHBOOK · IDEAS IN PROGRESS

ANOTHER WAY TO MAKE A MARK

I draw because stories can make us feel.

I want to make artwork that carries emotion, tells a story, and makes someone pause. I hope to bring that instinct for storytelling together with technology and other things I can do, to help people notice what matters and see things differently.

Those are intentions I'm still figuring out how to act on. I don't see technology as the destination. I see it as one vehicle for contributing to something bigger.

THE DESTINATION MUST MEAN SOMETHING

I'm not too small to make a difference.

I want my life to stand for people who are abused, neglected, unheard, or pushed to the margins. I hope to use technology, creativity, and whatever abilities I have to build useful things, solve real problems, and contribute to a kinder society.

I haven't arrived at that destination. I'm still growing as a developer and as a person. I don't want to become selfish, lose my convictions, or leave my potential unused. I want to keep learning, lead thoughtfully, and make the work serve people.

A NOTE TO MY FUTURE SELF

Keep your convictions close. Keep making room for other people.
